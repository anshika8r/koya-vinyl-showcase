# KOYA — Vinyl Product Showcase

KOYA is an interactive vinyl product showcase designed around the feeling of music after dark.

The experience explores different vinyl pressings, each representing a different mood and musical atmosphere.

## ✦ Features

- Four interactive vinyl pressings
- Individual product experiences for each pressing
- Interactive record details
- Hover interactions
- Scroll-based animation
- Responsive desktop and mobile experience
- Smooth product transitions and visual effects
- Dark editorial-inspired visual design

## 🎵 Pressings

### 01/ DANGER 🌑
A collection of dark, atmospheric sounds — slow rhythms, haunting melodies, and late-night frequencies made for listening after midnight.

### 02/ JUST ONE DAY 💟
Songs for the nights you fall in love, the mornings you remember, and everything your heart never learned to say.

### 03/ SPINE BREAKER ⛈️
Where the bass hits harder, the lights burn brighter, and the night moves faster.

### 04/ MAGIC SHOP 💜
For the lonely nights, the silent battles, and the beautiful process of finding your way back to yourself.
